Opening your neuron from another computer

This page is served over HTTPS, so your passphrase and keys cross the network encrypted. Your neuron made its own certificate for that, and no authority your browser knows signed it, so the browser warns you the first time (“Your connection is not private”). There are two safe ways past the warning.

1. Check the certificate, then go on

  1. On the machine your neuron runs on, in the folder you run satori from, print your neuron's certificate fingerprint: openssl x509 -in ./{{ instance }}/config/ui-cert.pem -noout -fingerprint -sha256 The neuron's log has it too: docker logs satori-{{ instance }} 2>&1 | grep fingerprint
  2. In the browser, click Not secure beside the address, open the certificate, and find its SHA-256 fingerprint.
  3. If the two match, go on (Advanced, then Proceed or Accept the risk). The browser remembers your choice for this address.
  4. If they don't match, or the warning comes back later when you haven't reset your neuron, someone may be between you and your neuron. Don't enter your passphrase.

On the computer the neuron runs on, nothing can come between you, so you can go on without checking.

2. Or come through an SSH tunnel

If you reach that machine over SSH, the tunnel carries this page inside SSH's own encryption. Run this on your own computer and leave it running:

ssh -N -L {{ port }}:localhost:{{ port }} you@{{ host }}

Then open https://localhost:{{ port }}. The browser still warns once, and through the tunnel you can go on without checking the fingerprint.

With the tunnel, the port needn't be open to the internet at all. Restart the neuron with it closed, and only that machine itself (so your tunnel) can reach this page:

SATORI_UI_BIND=127.0.0.1 ./satori {{ instance }} restart

Give it each time you restart, or the port opens again.
